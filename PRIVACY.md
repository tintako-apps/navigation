# Navigation – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026 · App version 1.0

## The short version

Navigation works on your device. Your location never leaves it. To download maps, the app connects to the servers of the CoMaps project, which see your IP address. Nothing reaches the developer. There is no account, no ads, no tracking and no Google services.

## Who is responsible

Tintako, Croatia · app@tintako.com

## What the app stores, and where

Only on your device, in the app's private storage:
- saved places (name, kind, address and coordinates);
- settings (language, voice, units, avoid options, the switches for driving and radio);
- while a drive is running, its destination and mode, so the drive can be resumed if the system stops the app; ending the drive or arriving deletes them;
- the time of the last check for newer maps (at most once a day);
- the maps you downloaded.

Backup and transfer to another device are turned off. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

## Permissions, and what each is for

- **Internet:** to download maps and to check for newer ones.
- **Network state:** to check for a connection before a download.
- **Precise and approximate location:** your position on the map, routes and guidance. Asked only when a screen needs it.
- **Extra location commands:** so the phone can refresh its satellite data (A-GPS), at most once every 16 hours and only with a network.
- **Foreground service and location service:** only with "Guidance while locked" switched on, from Go to the end of the drive.
- **Notifications:** the notice "Guiding to …" while guidance runs with the screen locked.

## What leaves, and only when you ask

- When you download a map, the app asks the CoMaps metaserver which map servers to use, and downloads the map file from one of them. The request contains the map name and version, the app's own name (User-Agent "Navigation/1.0 (github.com/tintako-apps/navigation)") and nothing about you or your location.
- When you open Settings → Maps with a network, at most once a day, the app asks the CoMaps servers which map versions exist. Nothing else is sent.
- Search, routes and guidance work on the device; your queries and routes are never sent.

## Who else receives anything

- **CoMaps servers** (comaps.app and the mirrors it lists): they see your IP address, use it to pick a nearby server, and some of them log requests. They receive no location.
- **Your phone's system:** for A-GPS it downloads satellite data from the chip maker's server; the app sends nothing to it.
- **The Radio app on the same device:** with "Radio while driving", the app asks it to play, pause or skip a station. Nothing leaves the device.

Nobody else. The developer receives nothing.

## Your choices

Downloads start only on a tap. Location is asked only when needed and can be refused or withdrawn in the system settings. Every switch that uses the battery is off by default.

## Children

The app is for a general audience and collects no personal data.
